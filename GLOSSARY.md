# Albarika Computer Institute

The institute trains students in IT and provides complete computing services — design, programming, typesetting, printing, exam preparation, and computer maintenance — at 56 Freeman Street, Lagos Island.

## Language

**Institute**:
Albarika Computer Institute, the organization.
_Avoid_: Computer Centre

### Training

**Course**:
A programme a student can enrol in, with a fixed duration and fee.
_Avoid_: program, school, class

**Enrolment request**:
A visitor's request to join a course. It opens nothing until staff approves it.
_Avoid_: registration, application, sign-up

**Enrolment**:
A student's place on one course, created when staff approves a request or registers someone at the desk.
_Avoid_: registration, subscription

**Student code**:
The unique code issued to each student (used as their login username alongside a password) and printed on their ID card (e.g. `ALB-2026-0042`).
_Avoid_: matric number, user ID

**Standing**:
What an enrolment is allowed to open: Partial (part of the fee received) or Complete (the whole fee received).
_Avoid_: approval level, status, tier

**Material**:
A course PDF document marked as opening at Partial or at Complete standing.
_Avoid_: content, resource, lesson

**ID card**:
The student's identity card, carrying their photo, name, student code, course, start date, and expiry date, downloaded as a PDF from the student portal.
_Avoid_: exam card, certification

**Certification**:
A completion document staff prints for a student from the admin. Distinct from the ID card.
_Avoid_: ID card, bill, certificate of payment

**Pricing bill**:
A printable page for one enrolment showing the course fee, the amount received, and the remaining balance.
_Avoid_: invoice, receipt

### Computing Services

**Computing services**:
The institute's work for clients and walk-in customers: document services & typesetting, design, printing, JAMB/WAEC online registration & CBT exam preparation, computer maintenance, data entry, and project creation.
_Avoid_: workshop-only

**Message**:
A contact-form submission from the website.
_Avoid_: ticket
